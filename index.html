Designers at work

This is a living collection of design practitioners exploring change-oriented possibilities for design. The list is compiled and updated regularly by students and teachers in the Department of Design at LNU. If interested in contributing, contact eric (dot) snodgrass (@) lnu.se. Original interface by Ariel Uzal (https://auzal.net/).

You can click on any tag in the table to filter by that specific design practice. Use this tool as a resource to explore practices across geographies and disciplines.
